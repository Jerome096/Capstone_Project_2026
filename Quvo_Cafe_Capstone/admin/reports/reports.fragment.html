How the colors work

Higher revenue, order counts and average paid order are green; lower values are red. For voided, unpaid and after-closing orders, fewer is green and more is red. Equal values or missing previous records are gray.

Daily compares the previous business day, Monthly the previous month, and Yearly the previous year. Each period follows its saved opening hours. Percent change is (selected value − previous value) ÷ previous value × 100. When the previous value is zero, the amount changes but no percentage is shown.
