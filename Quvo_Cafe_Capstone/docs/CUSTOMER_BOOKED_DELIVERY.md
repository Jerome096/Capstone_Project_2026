Two maximum-size images can exceed localStorage's capacity. Delivery submissions persist exact payload/photos in IndexedDB and place a small account-scoped pointer/cart in the existing durable pending-order store before sending the request. Accepted or definitively rejected submissions clear their stored proofs; logout preserves recovery under the original account. Ordinary cart recovery stays unchanged.
